Ice is a choice, not a test

Ice cools, dilutes and changes aroma release; decide what you want to preserve before choosing whether to add it, what ice and how much.

Shaking

A bartending technique in which ingredients and ice are shaken hard in a shaker to chill, dilute and mix the drink; compared with stirring, shaking adds more aeration and dilution.

A different glass, a different view

Glass shape changes how aroma gathers, where the liquid first meets your mouth and how fast it warms. The glass need not be expensive, only clean, suitable and sufficient.
