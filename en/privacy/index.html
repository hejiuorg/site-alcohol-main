Privacy

Privacy and data

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Hejiu is a static content site. It does not provide accounts, comments, purchases, bookings or cross-device sync. You can read it without signing in or submitting personal information.

What this site does not do

  • No advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels or third-party advertising profiles.
  • No reader accounts and no collection of names, phone numbers, addresses or payment details.
  • Site search runs in your browser; search terms are not uploaded to a server.
  • No sale or rental of reader data to third parties.

What stays in your browser

“Saved” items are written only to localStorage in this browser, remembering entity IDs, cached titles and timestamps. They do not sync across devices automatically. Clearing site data removes them; the saved page also offers export and import for your own backup.

Hosting and security logs

The site is served by static hosting and a CDN. The provider may record IP address, User-Agent, request time and status code for security, abuse prevention and aggregate statistics under its own privacy policy. Hejiu does not use these logs to profile readers or share them with ad networks.

No separate analytics product is connected at present. If one is added, it will be cookieless or privacy-friendly, and this page will be updated with the details and opt-out route.

External links and third-party content

Official sources, image licence pages and maps open third-party sites with their own privacy policies. Hejiu does not use those links to collect your data.

Contact and data requests

For privacy, source or removal questions, open an issue in the world-alcohol-encyclopedia repository with the page URL and your request. Code is MIT; original content is CC BY 4.0. Third-party standards, trademarks, photographs and quotations are not covered.
