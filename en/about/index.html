About 喝酒去

One content repository, one core repository and four sites, with one identity and evidence system.

Hejiu helps readers understand drinks, learn how to enjoy them, read a little culture, and find real places to visit. The four sites answer “what is this?”, “how do I enjoy it?”, “what stories sit beyond the glass?”, and “where can I go?”.

As of the latest build, the four sites publish 2960 pages (1480 Chinese and 1480 English) covering 1480 published entities. Whether an English page is public depends on its status: only reviewed translations whose source hash still matches enter the public index. Legacy archive records that could not stand alone are not published; old addresses are not redirected or rebuilt.

How content is organised

  • One content repository: world-alcohol-encyclopedia, with beverages, guide, culture, atlas and shared media.
  • One core repository: site-alcohol-core, carrying the builder, contracts, UI and brand assets; the four site repositories hold only apps.
  • hejiu.org carries ways to drink, saved items and site-wide search; drinks live at beverages.hejiu.org, with culture and places as their own sites.
  • Every object has a language-independent stable ID. Chinese and English are maintained separately, and each English file records the hash of the Chinese source it translated.
  • Relationships, sources, rights and translation status are checked by a real validator rather than guessed at build time.

What this edition covers

This site currently publishes 130 pages with no pending review previews. Live counts for every type are shown on the home and directory pages rather than hard-coded in prose.

Sources and limits

Every published page has at least one source independent of the old site; legacy text is no longer copied into public pages. Code is MIT and content is CC BY 4.0; third-party standards, trademarks, photographs and quoted texts are not covered by that licence.

Claims without checked sources are not presented as reviewed, and we do not invent brands, opening information, events or translations to fill pages. Current opening hours, ticket prices and booking rules should be checked with the venue's official channels.

This is a static site. It does not provide accounts, purchases, bookings or cross-device sync.

Contact, corrections and licence

For source additions, factual corrections or copyright issues, open an issue in the world-alcohol-encyclopedia repository with the page URL, evidence and rights-holder details. See Privacy for privacy and data details. Code is MIT; original content is CC BY 4.0. Reuse or adaptation should keep the “喝酒去 Hejiu” attribution, the original link and the licence note. Third-party standards, trademarks, photographs and quoted texts are not covered.
