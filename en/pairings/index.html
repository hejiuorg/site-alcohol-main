Thai cuisine with Riesling: high acid and aroma against heat

The sour heat, lemongrass, fish sauce and coconut milk of Thai food meet the high acidity and perfume of Riesling; choose dry to off-dry according to heat, sweetness and fat, not by "the sweeter the better". When ordering, check the dry or off-dry term, the alcohol level and the vintage, and serve at about 8 to 10 degrees Celsius.
